## 5. convert-text-to-numbers-dollar-signs-excel-google-sheets

Title candidates
- How to Convert Text With Dollar Signs to Numbers in Excel and Google Sheets (**Chosen**: action "convert", both apps, matches the pasted-amount case)
- How to Fix Numbers Stored as Text That Sum to Zero in Excel (matches "numbers stored as text sum zero", drops Google Sheets)
- How to Remove Dollar Signs and Commas From Text in Excel and Google Sheets (matches the SUBSTITUTE route)

First paragraph candidates
- A. (**Chosen**) Strip the symbols and convert: `=VALUE(SUBSTITUTE(SUBSTITUTE(TRIM(A2),"$",""),",",""))` turns the text "$1,250.00" into the number 1250. A column of pasted text amounts adds up to $0.00 until you do.
  - Why: exact formula and the symptom the searcher has, in two sentences.
- B. Use `=ISNUMBER(A2)` to check: FALSE means the amount is text, and SUM skips it.
  - Why: explains the diagnosis, but gives no fix.
- C. SUBSTITUTE removes the $ and commas, and VALUE turns what is left into a number.
  - Why: explains the parts, but is weaker without the full formula.
