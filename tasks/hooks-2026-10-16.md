## 1. week-ending-friday-date-weekday-excel-google-sheets

**Title (chosen):** How to Find the Week Ending Date for Any Date in Excel and Google Sheets

Rejected titles
- How to Group Hours by Week Ending Friday in Excel and Google Sheets (names the grouping, which is the Weeks tab, a second step after the date formula)
- How to Find the Friday of a Week in Excel and Google Sheets (fixes Friday in the title, but the template lets the reader choose the ending weekday)

**First paragraph (chosen):** Find the Friday that ends a date's week with `=A2+MOD(6-WEEKDAY(A2,1),7)`. A Wednesday such as 10/14/2026 returns 10/16/2026, and a Friday stays the same.

Backing: the MOD/WEEKDAY results (10/14 gives 10/16, 10/16 stays, 10/17 gives 10/23) were run in LibreOffice Calc 24.2 per the topics file. Confirm addresses against the built template (A2 is the log date).

## 2. free-shipping-threshold-amount-to-add-if-and-excel-google-sheets

**Title (chosen):** How to Check a Free Shipping Threshold and Show the Amount to Add in Excel and Google Sheets

Rejected titles
- How to Flag Orders That Qualify for Free Shipping in Excel and Google Sheets (covers the Free / Pay flag only and drops the amount to add)
- How to Use IF and AND to Test Two Conditions in Excel and Google Sheets (generic function search with no shipping task, so the download reads as off topic)

**First paragraph (chosen):** Label an order with `=IF(AND(B2="Ship",C2>=$G$1),"Free","Pay")`, then show how much more the customer needs to add with `=IF(AND(B2="Ship",C2<$G$1),MAX(0,$G$1-C2),0)`. Type your own threshold in G1; pickup orders never get free shipping.

Backing: IF/AND/MAX logic was run in LibreOffice Calc 24.2 per the topics file; no sample result is stated because the template data is not built yet. Confirm addresses against the built template (B2 delivery type, C2 subtotal, G1 threshold).

## 3. forecast-next-month-sales-forecast-linear-excel-google-sheets

**Title (chosen):** How to Forecast Next Month's Sales With FORECAST in Excel and Google Sheets

Rejected titles
- How to Project Sales From Past Months With a Trend Line in Excel and Google Sheets (vague on the function, and the template has no chart trend line)
- How to Use FORECAST.LINEAR for a Small Business in Excel and Google Sheets (the template uses the older FORECAST name, so this title would not match the formula shown)

**First paragraph (chosen):** Estimate the next month with `=FORECAST(A10,$B$2:$B$9,$A$2:$A$9)`, where A10 is the next month number, B2:B9 holds past sales and A2:A9 holds the month numbers. It fits a straight line through your past months, so it is an estimate, not a prediction.

Backing: FORECAST gave 1630 for the four points 1200, 1350, 1500 and 1480 in LibreOffice Calc 24.2 (topics file). That is a four month check, not the 8 month template, so no result is quoted here. Confirm addresses against the built template.

## 5. find-incomplete-customer-rows-countblank-excel-google-sheets

**Title (chosen):** How to Find Rows With Missing Information in a Customer List in Excel and Google Sheets

Rejected titles
- How to Count Blank Cells in Each Row in Excel and Google Sheets (right function, but no customer list or label task for the reader)
- How to Flag Customers With No Email or Address in Excel and Google Sheets (too narrow, since the template checks all five fields and names the first empty one)

**First paragraph (chosen):** Count the empty cells in each row with `=COUNTBLANK(A2:E2)`, then flag the row with `=IF(F2=0,"Complete","Missing")`. A cell that holds only a space does not count as blank.
