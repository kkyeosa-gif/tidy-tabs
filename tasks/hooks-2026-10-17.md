## 1. find-overlapping-bookings-date-ranges-countifs-excel-google-sheets

title: How to Find Overlapping Dates and Double Bookings in Excel and Google Sheets

opening: Count the other bookings for the same space that start on or before this booking's end date and end on or after its start date with `=COUNTIFS($B$2:$B$13,B2,$D$2:$D$13,"<="&E2,$E$2:$E$13,">="&D2)-1`. Any result above 0 is a double booking, and the `-1` removes the row itself.

## 2. cash-drawer-count-over-short-farmers-market-excel-google-sheets

title: How to Count a Cash Drawer and Find Over or Short in Excel and Google Sheets

opening: Multiply each denomination by its count, add the amounts with `=SUM(D2:D11)`, then find over or short with `=ROUND(D12-F4,2)`, where F4 is the starting float plus cash sales. A positive result is over, a negative result is short, and 0 is balanced.

search_description: Count a cash drawer and find over or short with =ROUND(D12-F4,2) in Excel and Google Sheets. Free cash count sheet for a market booth.

## 3. date-of-nth-weekday-in-month-second-saturday-excel-google-sheets

title: How to Find the Date of the 2nd Saturday (or Any Nth Weekday) of a Month in Excel and Google Sheets

opening: With the year in A2 and the month number in B2, `=DATE(A2,B2,1)+MOD(7-WEEKDAY(DATE(A2,B2,1),1),7)+7` returns the 2nd Saturday. Change the 7 inside MOD to another weekday number (1 is Sunday) and the last `+7` to `+7*(n-1)` for any other Nth weekday.

search_description: Find the 2nd Saturday of any month with =DATE(A2,B2,1)+MOD(7-WEEKDAY(DATE(A2,B2,1),1),7)+7 in Excel and Google Sheets. Free schedule.

## 4. format-phone-numbers-substitute-left-mid-right-excel-google-sheets

title: How to Format Phone Numbers in Excel and Google Sheets

opening: Strip spaces, hyphens, dots, parentheses and plus signs with nested SUBSTITUTE, then rebuild the 10 digits in C2 with `=IF(AND(LEN(C2)=10,ISNUMBER(--C2)),"("&LEFT(C2,3)&") "&MID(C2,4,3)&"-"&RIGHT(C2,4),"Check")`. A number like 5551234567 becomes (555) 123-4567, and anything that is not 10 digits shows Check.

search_description: Format phone numbers as (555) 123-4567 with ="("&LEFT(C2,3)&") "&MID(C2,4,3)&"-"&RIGHT(C2,4) in Excel and Google Sheets.

## 5. scale-recipe-batch-quantities-absolute-references-excel-google-sheets

title: How to Scale a Recipe Up or Down in Excel and Google Sheets

opening: Divide the new yield by the base yield to get a scale factor, then multiply each ingredient by it with the factor locked in place: `=ROUND(B6*$C$3,2)`. The `$` signs keep the reference on C3 when you fill the formula down the ingredient list.

search_description: Scale a recipe up or down with =ROUND(B6*$C$3,2) in Excel and Google Sheets. Absolute reference ($C$3) keeps the scale factor fixed. Free template.
