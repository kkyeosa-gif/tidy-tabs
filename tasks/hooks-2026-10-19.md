## 3. sum-with-or-condition-two-channels-sumproduct-excel-google-sheets

CHOSEN
title: How to Sum Sales From Either of Two Channels (OR Logic) in Excel and Google Sheets

opening: Add two SUMIFS together, one for each channel: `=SUMIFS(Orders!D:D,Orders!C:C,B1)+SUMIFS(Orders!D:D,Orders!C:C,B2)`, with the channel names typed in Totals B1 and B2. If both cells could ever hold the same channel, use `=SUMPRODUCT(((Orders!C2:C25=B1)+(Orders!C2:C25=B2)>0)*Orders!D2:D25)` so nothing is counted twice.

search_description: Sum sales from either of two channels with SUMIFS plus SUMIFS or SUMPRODUCT in Excel and Google Sheets. Free OR logic template with Etsy and Shopify sample orders.
