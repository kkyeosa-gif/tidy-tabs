To end up with a target amount after a percent fee plus a flat fee, divide instead of multiply: `=ROUND((B2+B4)/(1-B3),2)`, where B2 is the amount you want to keep, B3 the fee percent and B4 the flat fee. ## 4. pull-number-out-of-text-12-oz-value-left-find-excel-google-sheets

- C. How to Sum Cells That Contain Units Like oz or lb in Excel and Google Sheets (matches "sum cells with oz in them")

Openings
- A. For text like `12 oz` with a space, take everything before the space with `=VALUE(LEFT(TRIM(B5),FIND(" ",TRIM(B5)&" ")-1))`. For text like `12oz` with no space, strip the unit instead: `=VALUE(SUBSTITUTE(LOWER(TRIM(B5)),"oz",""))`. Either way you get a real number you can add up. (both shapes, both formulas)
- B. Text such as `12 oz` cannot be added until the unit is gone. Cut the number off at the first space, then use `VALUE` to turn it into a real number. (explains why, one method)
- C. `=SUMIF` and `=SUM` skip cells like `3 lb` because they are text. Strip the unit with `SUBSTITUTE`, convert with `VALUE`, and add the results. (starts from the failing sum)
