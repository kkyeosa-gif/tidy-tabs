## 1. weekly-sales-totals-from-daily-log-sumifs-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Add Up Weekly Sales From a Daily Sales Log in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen, the search wording). B. How to Total Sales by Week in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Group Daily Sales Into Weeks in Excel (vaguer).
Chosen opening: Add up one week of sales with `=SUMIFS(Sales!$C$5:$C$204,Sales!$A$5:$A$204,">="&A6,Sales!$A$5:$A$204,"<="&B6)`, where A6 is the Monday and B6 is `=A6+6`. Add 7 days for each row below to get the next week. (Exact formula and the week boundary a searcher needs.)

## 2. best-by-date-from-made-on-date-shelf-life-edate-excel-google-sheets
Title: How to Calculate a Best-By Date From the Made-On Date and Shelf Life in Excel and Google Sheets.
Chosen opening: Get a best-by date with `=EDATE(B6,C6)`, where B6 is the made-on date and C6 is the shelf life in months. Subtract an as-of date from the result to see how many days are left.

## 3. buy-2-get-1-free-price-calculator-int-excel-google-sheets
Title: How to Price Buy 2 Get 1 Free Deals in Excel and Google Sheets.
Chosen opening: For buy 2 get 1 free, count the free items with `=INT(B9/B6)*B4` (cart size, deal size of 3, free items per deal), then charge for the rest. A cart of 7 at $18.00 has 2 free items and costs $90.00.

## 4. flip-last-first-names-to-first-last-find-mid-excel-google-sheets
Title: How to Flip Last, First Names to First Last in Excel and Google Sheets.
Chosen opening: Turn `Alvarez, Maria` into `Maria Alvarez` with `=TRIM(MID(A5,FIND(",",A5)+1,200))&" "&TRIM(LEFT(A5,FIND(",",A5)-1))`. FIND locates the comma, MID takes the first name after it and LEFT takes the last name before it.

## 5. loyalty-punch-card-free-reward-tracker-countifs-excel-google-sheets
Title: How to Track a Loyalty Punch Card and Free Rewards in Excel and Google Sheets.
Chosen opening: Count each customer's punches with `=COUNTIFS(Purchases!$B$5:$B$304,A6,Purchases!$C$5:$C$304,">="&$B$3)`, then get the rewards earned with `=INT(B6/$B$2)`. Punches left to the next reward is `=$B$2-MOD(B6,$B$2)`.
