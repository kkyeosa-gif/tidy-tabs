## 1. shipping-charged-vs-label-cost-difference-sumif-excel-google-sheets

Shipping Cost Tracker for Etsy Orders in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Calculate Shipping Profit or Loss per Order in Excel (vaguer, drops Sheets).
Chosen opening: Subtract the label cost from the shipping you charged with `=ROUND(C5-D5,2)`. A negative result means the buyer's shipping did not cover the label. Add up only the shortfalls with `=ROUND(-SUMIF(E5:E204,"<0"),2)`. (Both formulas a searcher needs, in the order they use them.)

## 4. return-rate-by-product-sumifs-excel-google-sheets
Title: How to Calculate Return Rate by Product in Excel and Google Sheets.
Chosen opening: Divide units returned by units sold with `=C6/B6`, where each count comes from `=SUMIFS(Orders!$D$5:$D$304,Orders!$C$5:$C$304,A6)`. With 4 of 14 mugs returned, the return rate is 28.6%.

## 5. real-hourly-rate-fixed-price-project-sumifs-excel-google-sheets
Title: How to Find Your Real Hourly Rate on a Fixed-Price Project in Excel and Google Sheets.
Chosen opening: Divide the fixed price by the hours you logged with `=ROUND(B6/C6,2)`, where C6 is `=ROUND(SUMIFS(Time!$C$5:$C$404,Time!$B$5:$B$404,A6),2)`. A $2,400.00 project that took 52 hours paid $46.15 an hour.
