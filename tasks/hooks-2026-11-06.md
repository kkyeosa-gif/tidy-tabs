## 1. extract-text-between-parentheses-mid-find-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Extract Text Between Parentheses in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen, names the job and both apps). B. How to Get the Variation Out of an Etsy Listing Title in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Pull Text Inside Brackets in Excel (drops Sheets, and "brackets" is ambiguous).
Chosen opening: Pull the text inside the first pair of parentheses with `=TRIM(MID(A5,FIND("(",A5)+1,FIND(")",A5,FIND("(",A5))-FIND("(",A5)-1))`. For "Soy candle (Lavender, 8 oz)" it returns Lavender, 8 oz.

## 2. ship-date-daily-cutoff-time-workday-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Calculate a Ship Date With a Daily Cutoff Time in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. Order Cutoff Time Ship Date Formula in Excel (drops Sheets). C. How to Ship Next Business Day After 2 PM in Google Sheets (drops Excel, hard codes 2 PM).
Chosen opening: Find the day an order counts with `=IF(ROUND(MOD(B7,1)*86400,0)<=ROUND($B$2*86400,0),WORKDAY(INT(B7)-1,1,Holidays!$A$5:$A$24),WORKDAY(INT(B7),1,Holidays!$A$5:$A$24))`, then add the shipping days with `=WORKDAY(C7,$B$3,Holidays!$A$5:$A$24)`. With a 2:00 PM cutoff, an order at 5:05 PM on Wednesday 11/25/2026 ships Monday 11/30/2026.

## 3. change-due-count-bills-coins-int-sumproduct-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Calculate Change Due and Count Bills and Coins in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. Farmers Market Change Calculator in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Break Change Into Bills and Coins in Excel (drops Sheets).
Chosen opening: Get the change with `=ROUND(C8-B8,2)`, turn it into whole cents with `=ROUND(D8*100,0)`, then count each bill or coin with `=INT($E8/F$4)` and the matching formula in the next columns. A $31.35 sale paid with $50.00 gives $18.65 change: one $10, one $5, three $1 bills, two quarters, one dime and one nickel.

## 4. shipping-zone-from-zip-code-index-match-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Look Up a Shipping Zone From a ZIP Code in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. ZIP Code to Shipping Zone Lookup in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Find Shipping Price by ZIP Code in Excel (drops Sheets).
Chosen opening: Restore a ZIP that lost its zero with `=TEXT(C5,"00000")`, take the first three digits with `=VALUE(LEFT(D5,3))`, then look up the zone with `=INDEX(Zones!$B$5:$B$14,MATCH(E5,Zones!$A$5:$A$14,1))`. In the sample, 2108 becomes 02108 and lands in Zone 1 at $5.90 (a made-up zone table).

## 5. new-vs-returning-customers-by-month-minifs-countifs-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Count New and Returning Customers Each Month in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. New vs Returning Customers Tracker for Etsy Orders in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Tell If an Order Is From a New Customer in Excel (drops Sheets, drops the monthly count).
Chosen opening: Label an order New when its date equals the customer's earliest date, with `=IF(B6=E6,"New","Returning")`, where E6 is `=MINIFS($B$6:$B$305,$C$6:$C$305,C6)`. In the sample, October has 3 new customers and 8 of its 11 orders come from returning customers.
