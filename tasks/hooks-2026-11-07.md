## 1. 2026-11-07-team-01-split-file-name-and-extension-last-dot-substitute-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Split a File Name Into Name and Extension in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen, names the job and both apps). B. How to Get the Text After the Last Period in Google Sheets (drops Excel, abstract). C. Get a File Extension Formula in Excel (drops Sheets).
Chosen opening: Find the last dot with `=FIND(CHAR(1),SUBSTITUTE(B7,".",CHAR(1),LEN(B7)-LEN(SUBSTITUTE(B7,".",""))))`, then take the extension with `=MID(B7,C7+1,500)` and the name with `=LEFT(B7,C7-1)`. For "Invoice.2026.11.docx" the last dot is at position 16, the extension is docx and the name is Invoice.2026.11.

## 2. 2026-11-07-team-02-return-by-date-from-delivery-date-days-left-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Calculate a Return-By Date From the Delivery Date in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. Return Window Deadline Formula in Google Sheets (drops Excel). C. How to Add 30 Days to a Date in Excel (drops Sheets, drops days left).
Chosen opening: Add the return window to the delivery date with `=INT(B11)+D11`, then count the days left with `=E11-$B$3`, where B3 is an as-of date you type. A parcel delivered 10/20/2026 with a 30 day window is returnable until Thursday 11/19/2026, which is 4 days left as of 11/15/2026 (your own shop policy, not a legal rule).

## 3. 2026-11-07-team-03-look-up-price-by-part-of-product-name-wildcard-match-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Look Up a Price From Part of a Product Name in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. VLOOKUP Partial Match in Google Sheets (drops Excel, names a function the file does not use). C. How to Find a Product by Part of Its Name in Excel (drops Sheets, no price).
Chosen opening: Put a * on each side of the word you typed, so `=MATCH("*"&B9&"*",Products!$B$5:$B$104,0)` returns the first product whose name contains it, and `=INDEX(Products!$C$5:$C$104,D9)` returns its price. Typing tote finds "Canvas tote bag, 12 oz canvas" at $22.00 (fictional products).

## 4. 2026-11-07-team-04-customers-by-number-of-orders-frequency-table-countif-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Count Customers by Number of Orders in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen). B. How Many Customers Ordered More Than Once in Google Sheets (drops Excel, answers only one row of the table). C. Order Frequency Table for Etsy Customers in Excel (drops Sheets).
Chosen opening: Give each customer's first row a 1 with `=IF(SUMPRODUCT(--($C$5:C5=C5))=1,1,0)`, count their orders with `=SUMPRODUCT(--($C$5:$C$304=C5))`, then count customers who ordered exactly twice with `=COUNTIFS($E$5:$E$304,1,$D$5:$D$304,2)`. In the sample, 4 of 14 customers ordered twice.

## 5. 2026-11-07-team-05-wholesale-price-from-retail-price-margin-check-excel-google-sheets
Title candidates: A. How to Set a Wholesale Price From the Retail Price and Check Your Margin in Excel and Google Sheets (chosen, long but both halves of the job). B. Wholesale Price Calculator in Google Sheets (drops Excel, drops the margin check). C. How to Take 50 Percent Off Retail for Wholesale in Excel (drops Sheets, hard codes 50).
Chosen opening: Get the wholesale price with `=ROUND(C7*$B$2,2)` and your margin at that price with `=E7/D7`, where E7 is `=ROUND(D7-B7,2)`. At 50% of an $18.00 retail price the wholesale price is $9.00, and a $4.10 unit cost leaves a 54.4% margin (made-up numbers; the percent is yours to choose).
