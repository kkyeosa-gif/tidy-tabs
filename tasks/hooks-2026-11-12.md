- (chosen) Count the weekend and holiday days in a rental with `=E5-NETWORKDAYS(C5,C5+E5-1,Holidays!$A$4:$A$23)`, where C5 is the pickup date and E5 the days billed (`=MAX(1,D5-C5)`), then price it with `=ROUND(G5*$B$1+F5*$B$2,2)`. - To charge a weekend rate, split each rental into weekday days and weekend days, then multiply each by its rate. Why: plain, but no formula in the first sentence.
