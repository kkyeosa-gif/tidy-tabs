- (chosen) Count the subscribers active on the 1st of each month with `=COUNTIFS(start,"<"&m,cancel,"")+COUNTIFS(start,"<"&m,cancel,">="&m)`, which adds the ones with no cancel date to the ones who cancel on or after that day, then divide the people from that group who cancel during the month by it. Why: leads with the blank-only trap for the searcher whose COUNTIFS "blank or greater than date" search brought them here, rate second.
- If you sell a monthly candle, coffee or snack box and keep one row per subscriber with a start date and a cancel date, one formula per month can give you the starting count, new sign-ups, cancellations and churn rate without rebuilding the list. Why: situation first; no formula up front.
