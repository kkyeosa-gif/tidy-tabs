- Template: `templates/tidy-tabs-most-common-value.xlsx` (function `most_common_value`). Sheets: Orders, Summary (landscape), How to use. Print areas Orders A1:H50, Summary A1:G9. Best hero crop: Summary A1:G6 (most common item, times, share, tie message, quantity and the top 5 table), or Orders A1:D16 beside it.
- Inputs (yellow): Orders A2:C500 (Order, Item size or scent text, Quantity number). Sample: 40 fictional candle orders E-2001 to E-2040; items Lavender 11, Cedar 9, Vanilla 8, Citrus 7, Sandalwood 5; quantities from 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 (quantity 1 appears 13 times).
- Formulas (rows 2 to 500, all gray): D2 `=IF(B2="","",COUNTIF($B$2:$B$500,B2))` (exactly the hook's helper); E2 first-time flag `=IF(B2="","",IF(COUNTIF($B$2:B2,B2)=1,1,0))`; F2 sort key `=IF(E2=1,D2-ROW()/100000,"")`; G2 `=IF(ISNUMBER(C2),COUNTIF($C$2:$C$500,C2),"")`; H2 `=IF(ISNUMBER(C2),IF(COUNTIF($C$2:C2,C2)=1,1,0),"")`.
- Summary (labels A, values B): B1 orders counted `=SUMPRODUCT(--(Orders!$B$2:$B$500<>""))`; B2 most common item `=IFERROR(INDEX(Orders!$B$2:$B$500,MATCH(MAX(Orders!$D$2:$D$500),Orders!$D$2:$D$500,0)),"")` (exactly the hook's formula); B3 times it appears `=IF(B1=0,"",MAX(Orders!$D$2:$D$500))`; B4 share (0.0%); B5 tie message `COUNTIFS(D,B3,E,1)` more than 1 gives "N items tie at X orders; the first one listed is shown", else "No tie"; B6 most common quantity `=IF(COUNT(C)=0,"",IFERROR(MODE(Orders!$C$2:$C$500),"No repeats"))`; B7 times that quantity appears; B8 quantity tie message (same method with G and H); B9 CHECK `OK` when the first-time counts add up to B1, the quantity counts add up to the number of numeric quantities, every quantity is a number, and no quantity sits on a row with no item. Top 5 table D1:G6: rank D2:D6 (typed 1 to 5), item E2 `=IFERROR(INDEX(Orders!$B$2:$B$500,MATCH(LARGE(Orders!$F$2:$F$500,D2),Orders!$F$2:$F$500,0)),"")`, orders F2, share G2.
- Tie rule used by the template (describe this in the post): the tie test is a count of first-time rows (column E) that reach the max count; with a tie the item listed first is named and B5 says how many tie. No separate unique table: column E does that job.
- Recalculated sample (matches the Counter model): B1 40, B2 Lavender, B3 11, B4 27.5% (0.275), B5 No tie, B6 1, B7 13, B8 No tie, B9 OK. Top 5: Lavender 11 (27.5%), Cedar 9 (22.5%), Vanilla 8 (20.0%), Citrus 7 (17.5%), Sandalwood 5 (12.5%).
- Edge (edited copies, each matched the model): tie of two items (S and M at 2 each): S named, B5 "2 items tie at 2 orders; the first one listed is shown"; all items different: first listed named, "4 items tie at 1 orders", quantity "No repeats" (MODE's #N/A is caught, no error shown); one item only: 100%, no tie; blank rows in the middle: skipped, OK; Cedar, cedar, CEDAR: one item with 3, the spelling listed first (Cedar) shown; "Cedar " with a trailing space: a separate item (Cedar 2, "Cedar " 1, Vanilla 1); quantities typed as text ("2"): not counted by MODE, B9 CHECK; quantity on a row with no item: CHECK; item values that are numbers (12, 6): work, 6 named with 3; quantity tie (1, 2, 2, 1): B8 "2 quantities tie at 2 times; MODE returns one of them" and MODE returned 1, the one listed first (in the topics note MODE returned 12 of 12 and 24, also the one listed first; the tie rule of MODE itself is not documented here, say only what LibreOffice returned); empty Orders: zeros and blanks, B9 OK, no error; all 499 rows filled (rows 2 to 500) works; quantities all blank: B6 blank.
- Troubleshooting cases verified (LibreOffice only): "two values tie" = B5 message; "Cedar and cedar are counted together" = COUNTIF ignores case; "a value is counted separately" = trailing space; "quantity is ignored" = typed as text, B9 CHECK; "MODE shows No repeats" = every quantity different.
